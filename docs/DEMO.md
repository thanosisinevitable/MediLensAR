# Phone demo — after the Android build passes

These scenarios are prepared acceptance checks, not a claim that they ran here. Open Supported for the exact current formulations. Do not purchase or take medication for a demonstration. Use appropriately authorized packaging already available, or clearly mark any printed software fixture as a fixture; a print test is not a physical-package accuracy result.

1. **Camera and multiple regions.** Launch on an ARCore phone. Keep labels separated. Show live camera motion and compare label motion. Identify whether each annotation says DEPTH ATTACHED or SCREEN LABEL. Do not represent fallback as rigid 3D object tracking.
2. **Identification.** Hold a supported full brand/ingredient/strength/variant together. Watch unresolved → confirming → identified over three readings. Rotate or occlude the label and confirm evidence expires.
3. **Shared ingredient.** Present two independently identifiable supported packages with the same canonical ingredient. Review the shared-ingredient alert and explanation that observing two packages does not establish concurrent use.
4. **Known interaction.** If the two supported brand families for the one evidence-backed pair are available, show their resolved labels and the documented interaction. Open the source. Do not simulate this with a hidden synthetic production record.
5. **Open set.** Show an unsupported brand, incorrect strength, incomplete composition and a similar-looking word. Confirm UNRESOLVED and absence of identity-based clinical assertions.
6. **Plan.** Enter a date/time yourself, inspect the original timezone and record a taken/skipped event. Relaunch and confirm encrypted data persistence. Delete the occurrence and confirm removal.
7. **Offline.** After AR Services setup, enable airplane mode and separately ensure Wi-Fi is off. Force-stop/relaunch. Repeat camera, OCR, identification, SQLite reasoning, plan and AR checks. Record actual behavior; no offline result is currently claimed.

No demo buttons inject invented medicines into the live scanner. The synthetic domain integration fixtures are confined to `tests/CoreTests.java` and do not ship in app assets.
